Doses are calculated for {{ t.vol }} gal. Rates marked estimate are general spa ratios; the rest come off the AquaDoc label.

Spa Time Bromine Granules Estimate

Sanitizer — routine top-up

Granules. ½ tsp raises bromine about 1 ppm in 290 gal. Broadcast over the surface with the jets running, cover off 15 min.

Bromine Booster Estimate

Builds the bromide bank after a refill

Granules. 2 tbsp per fresh fill, then activate with Oxidizing Shock. Not part of weekly dosing.

Oxidizing Shock Estimate

Activates bromide, burns off waste

Granules. 1 tbsp weekly, or after heavy use. Leave the cover off until bromine falls below 10 ppm.

pH Increaser Estimate

Raises pH

Liquid. Blower or filter on, add 1 oz, retest after 30 minutes. Repeat until pH is 7.2–7.8. Never more than 1 oz per dose.

pH Decreaser AquaDoc label

Lowers pH, and the only lever for high alkalinity

Liquid. Use when pH is above 7.8. Blower or filter on, add 1 oz, retest after 30 minutes. Repeat until pH is 7.2–7.8 and alkalinity 80–120 ppm. Never more than 1 oz at a time.

Alkalinity Booster Estimate

Raises total alkalinity

Granules. 1½ tbsp raises alkalinity about 10 ppm in 290 gal. Always before pH — it moves pH up with it.

Calcium Increaser AquaDoc label

Raises calcium hardness

Granules. 1 level capful (2 tbsp) raises hardness 10 ppm in 500 gal — about 1 tbsp per 10 ppm at 290 gal. Add with the equipment on, run the filter 1–2 hours, retest after 20 minutes.

Order of operations: alkalinity, then calcium, then pH, then bromine — circulating and retesting between stages.
